## Background/Context

OCHRE has a `RoomAC` class (window air conditioners) that sets `duct_dse = 1.0` and uses different airflow assumptions than central AC. RoomAC is present in ~15% of ResStock housing stock. HPXML files with room ACs currently fail to resolve to the correct equipment type or fall through to central AC (wrong duct DSE, wrong airflow).

## Work to Do

- [ ] Implement `RoomAC` as a variant of the existing air conditioner, with:
  - `duct_dse = 1.0` (no duct losses — directly conditions the zone)
  - Airflow rate: 320 CFM/ton (manufacturer data median, AHRI 310/380 conditions; lower than central AC 400 CFM/ton due to compact cross-flow blowers)
  - No duct distribution zone routing (single-zone only)
- [ ] Ensure HPXML room AC system type maps to this equipment correctly in the equipment resolver
- [ ] Register in equipment catalog

## Files to Touch

- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/air_conditioner.rs`: Add RoomAC configuration path or variant
- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/mod.rs`: Registration

## Tests Added

**hares-equipment:**

- `room_ac_duct_dse_is_one` — construct a RoomAC. Assert `duct_dse == 1.0`
- `room_ac_step_produces_cooling` — step RoomAC with cooling demand. Assert negative sensible gain (cooling) and positive electrical consumption
- `room_ac_no_duct_loss_zone` — assert RoomAC does not create a separate duct loss zone entry in thermal port writes
- `room_ac_vs_central_ac_different_dse` — same capacity/EIR config as central AC but as RoomAC. Assert RoomAC delivers more cooling to the zone (no duct loss)
- `room_ac_airflow_rate` — assert airflow rate matches expected CFM/ton for window unit

## Measures of Success
