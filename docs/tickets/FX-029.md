## Background/Context

OCHRE captures `Number of Bedrooms`, `Number of Bathrooms`, `House Type`, `Total Floors`, `Indoor Floors` in its construction dict. HARES stores none of these, forcing every consumer to re-parse raw XML. These block type-safe hot water draw calculations and infiltration models.

## Work to Do

- [ ] Add to `Building` struct:
  - `n_bedrooms: Option<f64>` (not `u32` — all downstream arithmetic is `f64`; avoids casts everywhere)
  - `n_bathrooms: Option<f64>` with OCHRE fallback `n_beds / 2 + 0.5` when absent (BA Benchmark)
  - `residential_facility_type: Option<ResidentialFacilityType>` as a **typed enum** (`SingleFamilyDetached`, `SingleFamilyAttached`, `ManufacturedHome`, `ApartmentUnit`, `Other(String)`) — not `Option<String>`
  - `n_floors: Option<u32>`, `n_floors_above_grade: Option<u32>` (total floors is primary, above-grade is secondary — match OCHRE's precedence)
- [ ] Parse from HPXML: `<NumberofBedrooms>`, `<NumberofBathrooms>`, `<ResidentialFacilityType>`, `<NumberofConditionedFloors>`, `<NumberofConditionedFloorsAboveGrade>`
- [ ] **Remove duplicate parsing in `equipment.rs`**: replace the `n_bedrooms` re-parse at lines 511-517 and 935-943, and the `n_floors` re-parse at lines 620-635, with reads from the `Building` struct
- [ ] Note: `residential_facility_type` is the gating dependency for OCHRE-equivalent occupancy-adjusted bedrooms formula (hpxml.py:790-798) — track that as follow-on
- [ ] Add tests with fixture files
