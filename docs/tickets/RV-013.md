## Background/Context

The existing EV driver test (`test_steps_without_error` in `test_ev_archetypes.py`) only runs 3 steps — far too few to exercise the departure-drive-arrival-plug-in lifecycle. `DayEvent::departure_minute` is typically 480 (8 AM), meaning phase transitions occur only after 8+ hours of simulation at 1-minute resolution. No test verifies the complete EV lifecycle.

## Work to Do

- [ ] Add a Rust-level integration test that runs a full 24+ hour simulated day
- [ ] Add a Python-level test that runs enough steps to cover the complete cycle
- [ ] Verify SOC transitions, connection state changes, and energy accounting

## Tests Added

All multi-step EV tests should use `#[timeout(30_000)]` or equivalent to prevent CI hangs if the step loop diverges.

**hares-equipment (ev/tests.rs):**

- `ev_full_day_lifecycle` — configure EV with departure at minute 480, arrival at minute 1080 (6 PM). Run 1440+ steps (24 hours). Assert:
  - Steps 0-479: `connection_state == HomePluggedIn`, SOC increases (charging)
  - Steps 480-1079: `connection_state == Driving` or `Away`, SOC decreases proportional to `fuel_economy × distance`
  - Steps 1080+: `connection_state == HomePluggedIn`, SOC increases (post-arrival charging)
- `ev_soc_decreases_during_driving` — run through a departure event. Assert SOC at arrival < SOC at departure by expected amount based on trip distance and fuel economy
- `ev_connection_state_transitions` — track connection_state over a full day. Assert exactly the expected sequence: HomePluggedIn → Driving → (possibly AwayPluggedIn) → HomePluggedIn
- `ev_energy_accounting_closed` — sum all energy in (charging kWh) and energy out (driving kWh). Assert energy balance closes within 1% of battery capacity
- `ev_departure_at_step_boundary` — departure at exact step 1440 (end of day). Assert transition still occurs correctly at the boundary
- `ev_insufficient_charge_before_departure` — configure low initial SOC and short window before departure. Assert EV departs with whatever SOC it has (no panic, no infinite charging loop)
- `ev_soc_curve_monotonic_during_charging` — sample SOC at 10-step intervals during charging phase. Assert monotonically non-decreasing

**tests/python/test_ev_archetypes.py:**

- `test_ev_full_day_cycle` — run 1500 steps (25 hours). Assert SOC at step 0 vs step 1440 shows charging occurred, and SOC at mid-day shows discharge from driving. Assert intermediate SOC values are bounded in [0, 1]
