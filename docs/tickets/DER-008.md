## Background/Context

DER_Detection has a rich catalog of 11 commercial battery products with real specs. Users need factory methods to create pre-configured batteries, but also retain the ability to construct arbitrary batteries with custom parameters via the `Battery(...)` constructor.

Product IDs live in `hares-equipment` (catalog concern), not `hares-types` (domain concept).
