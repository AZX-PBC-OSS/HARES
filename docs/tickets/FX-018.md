## OCHRE Divergence Note

OCHRE only applies its safety cutout in the duty-cycle control path, not every step. HARES applies it unconditionally in `update_control`, which is strictly better protection coverage. Both OCHRE and HARES share the same class of bug (checking a single node rather than max), but after this fix HARES will be superior.
