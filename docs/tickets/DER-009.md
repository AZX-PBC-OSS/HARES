## Background/Context

DER_Detection defines 13 EV models (10 BEV + 3 PHEV) with real specs. Users need factory methods to create pre-configured EV equipment from known vehicles, but also retain the ability to construct arbitrary EVs via the `EV(...)` constructor with custom parameters.

Vehicle IDs live in `hares-equipment` (catalog concern), not `hares-types`.
