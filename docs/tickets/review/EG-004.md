## Background/Context

OCHRE back-calculates garage wall height from `sqrt(a1 * a2 / garage_floor_area)`.
For 2-wall garages: `garage_area_in_main = a1 * a2 / garage_wall_height²`. This
affects thermal zone sizing.

## Investigation Instructions

1. Read `building.rs` garage geometry section.
2. Compare with OCHRE `parse_hpxml_zones()` garage geometry (line ~440).
3. Document any differences or simplifications.


## Findings

### Summary

HARES derives garage geometry directly from the `<Garage><FloorArea>` HPXML element (`building.rs:1399-1400`). OCHRE also reads `<Garage Floor>` slab area (`hpxml.py:441`) but then performs an extensive back-calculation from wall geometry to derive `garage_wall_height`, `garage_area_in_main`, and `garage_protruded_area`. HARES skips all of that back-calculation entirely.

Three concrete defects result.

---

### FINDING 1 — `garage_protruded_area` is never computed (Severity: High)

**OCHRE** (`hpxml.py:449-494`): derives `garage_wall_height` from the perpendicular pair of exterior garage wall areas, then uses the count and azimuths of attached-wall segments to determine how much of the garage footprint is tucked inside the main building rectangle vs. protruding. The result is:

```
garage_protruded_area = garage_floor_area - garage_area_in_main
```

This quantity feeds two downstream consumers:
1. The combined attic volume formula for 2-story homes with a protruding garage (`hpxml.py:619-631`): the attic volume is split into a prism over the square footprint, a prism over the garage, and a pyramid between them. Without `garage_protruded_area` that decomposition is impossible.
2. The garage roof-space volume addition (`hpxml.py:732-734`): `garage_volume += 0.5 * atan(garage_tilt) * garage_protruded_area`.

**HARES** (`building.rs:677-679`): garage volume is simply `floor_area * ceiling_height`. There is no `garage_protruded_area`, no `garage_tilt`, and no roof-space addend. For ResStock cases (where ~50% of the garage protrudes), this underestimates garage volume by several cubic metres.

**Impact**: wrong thermal zone volume → wrong heat capacity → wrong zone temperature dynamics for garages with pitched roofs.

**Evidence**: `building.rs:677-679`, `vendors/OCHRE/ochre/utils/hpxml.py:730-734`.

---

### FINDING 2 — Attic volume formula drops the garage correction for 3-gable homes (Severity: High)

**OCHRE** (`hpxml.py:602-631`): when the attic has 3 gable walls (a standard pair plus a garage gable), it computes the attic volume in three additive parts:

```python
attic_volume = (
    0.5 * square_area * attic_height              # prism over the square
  + 0.5 * garage_protruded_area * garage_height   # prism over garage
  + 1/6 * garage_width * garage_depth_in_house * garage_height  # pyramid
)
```

**HARES** (`compute_attic_volume`, `building.rs:1927-1943`): when 3 gable walls are present, HARES picks the median gable area and uses the simple formula `0.5 * floor_area * attic_height`. The pyramid term and the garage prism are both absent, and `floor_area` used here is the full attic floor area (which already includes `garage_protruded_area` per OCHRE's own `attic_floor_area` construction), so the simple formula over-counts.

For a standard 2-story ResStock home with a protruding garage the error can be 5-15% of total attic volume, biasing attic temperature and heat transfer through the ceiling.

**Evidence**: `building.rs:1927-1943`, `hpxml.py:607-631`.

---

### FINDING 3 — Ceiling height fallback of 2.5 m diverges from OCHRE (Severity: Medium)

**OCHRE**: `garage_volume = garage_floor_area * ceiling_height` where `ceiling_height` is derived from `ConditionedBuildingVolume / ConditionedFloorArea` (`hpxml.py:529-530`). When the conditioned volume element is absent OCHRE would fail to parse rather than silently use a fallback.

**HARES** (`building.rs:672-679`): uses `default_height_m = ceiling_height_m.unwrap_or(2.5)` for all non-attic zones including the garage. The 2.5 m value is below typical US residential ceiling heights (~2.74 m / 9 ft). When `<ConditionedBuildingVolume>` is absent from HPXML, HARES silently computes a smaller garage (and conditioned zone) volume than OCHRE would on the same input, with no warning emitted.

**Evidence**: `building.rs:672`.

---

### FINDING 4 — `garage_wall_height` back-calculation is absent (Severity: Medium, prerequisite for F1)

OCHRE derives wall height geometrically from perpendicular wall areas because HPXML does not store ceiling height per-zone:

```python
a1, a2 = max-area-walls-per-azimuth-pair
garage_wall_height = sqrt(a1 * a2 / garage_floor_area)
```

This is needed to convert attached-wall areas into a footprint dimension for `garage_area_in_main`. HARES has no equivalent. The wall height derivation must be implemented before Findings 1 and 2 can be fixed.

**Evidence**: `hpxml.py:449-455`, `building.rs` (absent).
