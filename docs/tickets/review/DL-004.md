## Background/Context

OCHRE reports duct losses as `|delivered_heat| * (1 - duct_dse)` in the
`"{end_use} Duct Losses (W)"` output column. HARES should report equivalent.

## Investigation Instructions

1. Read OCHRE duct loss reporting.
2. Read HARES — is duct loss reported in telemetry?
3. Is the formula correct? Is it absolute value or signed?
4. Is duct loss also reported via a `DuctLoss` thermal port contribution?

_To be filled by investigating agent. Must include specific, named tests with assertions._

## Findings

### Summary

Three distinct bugs are present. `ThermalCategory::DuctLoss` is defined and wired
into the output pipeline but is **never emitted by any equipment**. As a consequence
`duct_loss_w` is structurally always 0 in every output row.

Additionally, `duct_loss_w` is measured from the wrong zone accumulator, and the
duct zone thermal contributions are tagged with the wrong category.

---

### Finding 1 — CRITICAL BUG: `ThermalCategory::DuctLoss` never emitted by any equipment

**Severity: Critical (incorrect output)**

Every call site of `write_zone_thermal_contributions` passes either
`ThermalCategory::HvacHeating` or `ThermalCategory::HvacCooling` — including the
contribution that is routed to the duct zone (i.e. the `1 - dse` fraction):

- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/furnace.rs:165,369` — `ThermalCategory::HvacHeating`
- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/heat_pump/heater.rs:591` — `ThermalCategory::HvacHeating`
- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/air_conditioner.rs:637` — `ThermalCategory::HvacCooling`
- `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/baseboard.rs:125` — `ThermalCategory::HvacHeating`

`write_zone_thermal_contributions` in `duct_distribution.rs` distributes heat to
both the conditioned zone (fraction = `dse`) and the duct zone (fraction = `1 - dse`)
using **the same `category` argument** passed by the caller.

`ThermalCategory::DuctLoss` is never passed as a `category` argument anywhere in the
equipment crate. The enum variant exists, the accumulator index is correct (index 4),
and the output pipeline reads it from the indoor accumulator — but no data ever
arrives there.

**Fix required:** `write_zone_thermal_contributions` must apply the caller-supplied
category to the conditioned zone contribution, and `ThermalCategory::DuctLoss` to the
duct zone contribution. The method signature already receives `category`; the duct-zone
branch must override it:

```rust
// conditioned zone entry
category,                      // HvacHeating or HvacCooling — keep as-is

// duct zone entry
ThermalCategory::DuctLoss,     // always DuctLoss for the loss fraction
```

---

### Finding 2 — BUG: `duct_loss_w` read from indoor zone accumulator, not duct zone accumulator

**Severity: High (even after Finding 1 is fixed, duct_loss_w will still be 0)**

`crates/hares-envelope/src/thermal_solver/mod.rs:376-378` reads `duct_loss_w` from
`indoor_acc` — the accumulator for the indoor/conditioned zone:

```rust
let duct_loss_w = indoor_acc
    .map(|a| a.sensible_for_category(ThermalCategory::DuctLoss))
    .unwrap_or(0.0);
```

Duct losses are energy deposited into the **duct zone** (attic, crawlspace, garage),
not into the conditioned indoor zone. Even if Finding 1 is fixed and equipment emits
`ThermalCategory::DuctLoss` contributions, those contributions will be accumulated
into the duct zone's `ThermalAccumulator`, not the indoor zone. Reading `indoor_acc`
for `DuctLoss` will therefore always return 0.

**OCHRE context:** OCHRE reports `|delivered_heat| * (1 - duct_dse)` as a scalar
derived from the capacity computation — it is not read back from a zone accumulator.
HARES attempts a different approach (read the tagged category from the accumulator),
but reads the wrong accumulator.

**Fix required:** Either:
(a) Read `duct_loss_w` from the duct zone accumulator (requires knowing the duct zone
id inside the thermal solver, which may not currently be available), or
(b) Compute it as a derived scalar: `gross_capacity_w * (1.0 - dse)` alongside the
`hvac_heating_w` / `hvac_cooling_w` values, mirroring OCHRE's approach.

Option (b) is simpler and avoids the wrong-accumulator ambiguity.

---

### Finding 3 — MODERATE BUG: duct zone receives `HvacHeating`/`HvacCooling` category, polluting heating/cooling delivered totals

**Severity: Moderate (correctness — hvac_heating_w and hvac_cooling_w are overstated)**

Because every contribution is tagged `HvacHeating` or `HvacCooling` (Finding 1),
the duct zone's heat gain appears in `hvac_heating_w` / `hvac_cooling_w` totals.
These totals are used to compute `envelope_hvac_heating_wh` / `envelope_hvac_cooling_wh`
in `crates/hares-io/src/output/metrics.rs`.

When a duct zone exists (e.g. vented attic), the metrics over-report HVAC delivered
energy by the duct loss fraction. This inflates `HVAC Heating Delivered (W)` and
`HVAC Cooling Delivered (W)` output columns.

OCHRE makes the distinction explicit: `delivered_heat * duct_dse` = delivered,
`delivered_heat * (1 - duct_dse)` = losses. HARES conflates both into the same
category.

**Fix required:** Resolved by applying Finding 1 fix — once the duct zone fraction is
tagged `DuctLoss`, `hvac_heating_w` (read from indoor zone, `HvacHeating` category)
will correctly represent only the energy actually delivered to the conditioned space.

---

### Finding 4 — INFORMATIONAL: `duct_loss_w` comment in `EnvelopeComponentGains` is misleading

**Severity: Low (documentation)**

`crates/hares-envelope/src/thermal_solver/config.rs:450-451`:
```rust
/// Duct distribution losses to the indoor zone [W].
pub duct_loss_w: f64,
```

The doc comment says "to the indoor zone" but duct losses are by definition heat
deposited into an unconditioned duct zone — they are NOT a gain to the indoor zone.
The field semantics should be "duct distribution losses [W] — heat deposited into
the duct zone, removed from delivered capacity".

---

### Finding 5 — INFORMATIONAL: Test in `hvac_core.rs` demonstrates wrong category without asserting it

**Severity: Low (test gap)**

`crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/hvac_core.rs:1857`
(`write_zone_thermal_contributions_routes_duct_loss_to_duct_zone`) verifies that
the duct zone receives `gross * (1 - DSE)` watts of sensible heat, but does not
assert the thermal category of that contribution. When Finding 1 is fixed, this
test must be updated to assert `duct_zone_acc.sensible_for_category(DuctLoss) == 3000.0`
and `duct_zone_acc.sensible_for_category(HvacHeating) == 0.0`.

Similarly the conditioned-zone entry should be asserted to have `HvacHeating == 7000.0`
and `DuctLoss == 0.0`.
