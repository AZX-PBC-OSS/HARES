## Background/Context

Fan motor heat is both an electrical load (increases total kW) and a thermal gain
to the conditioned zone (for indoor fan motors, ~100% of fan energy becomes heat).
OCHRE accounts for this in both paths. HARES must do the same.

## Investigation Instructions

1. In HARES HVAC `step()`, trace: where is fan power added to electrical port?
2. Where is fan motor heat added to thermal port?
3. For heating: fan heat supplements heating (net benefit).
4. For cooling: fan heat opposes cooling (net penalty).
5. Is this sign convention correct in HARES?


## Recommended Tests

- `air_conditioner_fan_heat_in_thermal_port`: At full duty, assert that `ports.thermal[0].sensible_gain_w == -(sensible_cooling_w - fan_heat_w)`, i.e. the net zone gain is less negative than gross sensible cooling by exactly `fan_kw * 1000.0`.
- `heat_pump_heater_fan_heat_in_thermal_port`: At full duty with a known `fan_power_w`, assert that `ports.thermal[0].sensible_gain_w == hp_capacity_w + fan_power_w` (no ER active, no defrost).
- Extend `gas_furnace_energy_balance_tracks_fuel_plus_fan` to also assert `ports.thermal[0].sensible_gain_w == gross_capacity_w + fan_heat_w` (confirm the reference case is tested end-to-end).

## Findings

### STATUS: TWO CONFIRMED BUGS

---

### Finding 1 — BUG: `AirConditioner` omits fan heat from zone thermal port

**File:** `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/air_conditioner.rs:637–642`

```rust
self.hvac.write_zone_thermal_contributions(
    ports,
    -sensible_cooling_w,   // fan_kw offset NOT applied
    -latent_cooling_w,
    ThermalCategory::HvacCooling,
)?;
```

`fan_kw` is computed (line 606, scaled at line 632) and flows into the electrical port via `electric_kw = compressor_kw + fan_kw + crankcase` (line 666–667). It is never written to the zone thermal port.

OCHRE (`HVAC.py:543`): `sensible_gain = heat_gain * shr + fan_power`. For cooling, `heat_gain < 0`, `fan_power > 0`, so the fan partially offsets the cooling extraction before the net gain reaches the zone. HARES writes the full gross `-(sensible_cooling_w)`, biasing the zone cold by `fan_heat_w` every step.

Magnitude: 3-ton AC, default 400 CFM/ton, 0.365 W/CFM → `fan_power ≈ 438 W` continuous bias during cooling.

**Fix:**
```rust
self.hvac.write_zone_thermal_contributions(
    ports,
    -sensible_cooling_w + fan_kw * 1000.0,
    -latent_cooling_w,
    ThermalCategory::HvacCooling,
)?;
```
`fan_kw` is already scaled by `thermal_ratio * effective_load` at line 632, keeping it consistent with the scaled cooling load.

---

### Finding 2 — BUG: `HeatPumpHeater` omits fan heat from zone thermal port

**File:** `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/heat_pump/heater.rs:817`

```rust
let mut thermal_output_w = hp_capacity_w + er_capacity_w;
// fan_power_w is excluded — only included in electric_kw at line 818
let mut electric_kw = (hp_electric_w + er_power_w + fan_power_w + pan_heater_w) / 1000.0;
```

Fan motor waste heat is drawn electrically and dissipates into the duct airstream (sensible gain to zone), but is absent from `thermal_output_w`. The zone receives only compressor + ER heat; fan heat is silently discarded.

Magnitude: 3-ton ASHP, same sizing → `fan_power_w ≈ 438 W` missing from zone every heating step. `fan_power_w` is already zeroed when `hp_on` is false (line 796), so adding it is safe.

**Fix:** In `compute_step`, add `fan_power_w` to `thermal_output_w` before control scaling:
```rust
let mut thermal_output_w = hp_capacity_w + er_capacity_w + fan_power_w;
```
This must be placed before the `effective_load` and `ctrl_power_limit_kw` scaling blocks so fan heat is uniformly attenuated with compressor output under demand response and power limiting.

---

### Finding 3 — CORRECT: `GasFurnace` is the reference implementation

**File:** `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/furnace.rs:364–374`

```rust
let fan_heat_w = fan_kw * 1000.0;
let total_sensible_w = gross_capacity_w + fan_heat_w;
self.hvac.write_zone_thermal_contributions(ports, total_sensible_w, 0.0, ThermalCategory::HvacHeating)?;
```

Correct. Fan heat is in both electrical and thermal ports. Findings 1 and 2 should be aligned to this pattern.

---

### Finding 4 — ARCHITECTURAL NOTE: `ElectricFurnace` lacks a separate fan model

**File:** `crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/furnace.rs:154–170`

`ElectricFurnace` uses `eir = 1.0` and routes all electrical draw directly to `gross_capacity_w` written to the thermal port. There is no `fan_power_w` field. At `eir = 1.0`, energy balance holds (all electric = heat out including fan). However, if `eir < 1.0` is ever configured (to model an imperfect element), fan heat would silently vanish from the zone. Not a blocking defect at current usage, but the `GasFurnace` decomposition (explicit `fan_power_w` field, separate thermal contribution) is the better pattern and should be adopted if this equipment ever gains configurable efficiency.

---

### Review Note — Coil Entering Temperature vs Additive Fan Heat

The AC's `calculate_performance` already adjusts `coil_input_db` upward by fan heat (`air_conditioner.rs:782–799`) — this affects the biquadratic capacity/EIR lookup, reducing gross capacity somewhat. However, OCHRE also does this (`HVAC.py:475–477`) AND still adds `fan_power` additively to `sensible_gain` at line 543. The two effects are not energy-equivalent: the coil temperature adjustment is a second-order effect on capacity; the additive term is the first-order direct heat injection. HARES correctly adjusts coil entering temperature but misses the additive term.

## Review Notes

AC uses fan shaft heat to adjust coil entering temperature (multiplicative via biquadratic). OCHRE adds fan power unconditionally to sensible_gain (additive). These are different mechanisms — check whether they are energy-equivalent under capacity clipping.

**Resolution:** They are NOT equivalent and are not alternatives. OCHRE does both. HARES does only the coil temperature adjustment. The additive term must also be added (Finding 1).
