## Background/Context

OCHRE derives multiple floor area values: `first_floor_area`, `top_floor_area`,
`attic_floor_area`, `indoor_floor_area`. These feed into zone volumes and surface
assignments.

## Investigation Instructions

1. Read OCHRE floor area derivation (lines 419-496).
2. Read HARES `building.rs` equivalent logic.
3. Compare each derived area: same surfaces used? Same formulas?
4. Check `indoor_floor_area = conditioned_floor_area - first_floor_area * (total_floors - indoor_floors)`.


## Recommended Tests

1. **`test_conditioned_zone_area_excludes_foundation`** — Parse a 2-story HPXML with a finished
   basement (`NumberofConditionedFloors=2`, `NumberofConditionedFloorsAboveGrade=1`,
   `ConditionedFloorArea=200 m²`, `Foundation/FloorArea=100 m²`). Assert that the Conditioned
   zone `floor_area_m2 == 100.0` (above-grade only) and the Foundation zone
   `floor_area_m2 == 100.0`.

2. **`test_conditioned_zone_area_slab_passthrough`** — Parse a single-story slab-on-grade HPXML
   (`NumberofConditionedFloors=1`, `ConditionedFloorArea=150 m²`). Assert conditioned zone
   `floor_area_m2 == 150.0` (no subtraction needed).

3. **`test_conditioned_zone_volume_multi_story`** — For the 2-story basement case above with
   `ConditionedBuildingVolume` set, verify zone volume equals `indoor_floor_area * ceiling_height`
   not `conditioned_floor_area * ceiling_height`.

## Findings

### Finding 1 — CRITICAL: Conditioned zone floor area includes underground foundation area

**Location:** `building.rs:410-412, 568, 1342`

OCHRE (`hpxml.py:253, 482`) explicitly notes `ConditionedFloorArea` is "indoor + foundation" and
subtracts the underground contribution before assigning the conditioned zone area:

```python
conditioned_floor_area = convert(construction["ConditionedFloorArea"], "ft^2", "m^2")  # indoor + foundation
# ...
indoor_floor_area = conditioned_floor_area - first_floor_area * (total_floors - indoor_floors)
zones["Indoor"]["Zone Area (m^2)"] = indoor_floor_area
```

HARES passes the raw HPXML value directly:

```rust
let conditioned_floor_area_m2 = ...  // ConditionedFloorArea — includes foundation
// build_zone_map assigns it directly to the conditioned zone
floor_area_m2: conditioned_floor_area_m2,
```

For any building with a conditioned or unconditioned basement the basement footprint is counted
twice: once in the conditioned zone and again in the foundation zone. This inflates:
- Conditioned zone thermal mass (interior wall area = `floor_area_m2`, furniture area = fraction of that).
- Conditioned zone volume (`floor_area_m2 * ceiling_height_m`), slowing thermal response.

**Fix:** After `build_zone_map` returns, compute
`indoor_floor_area = conditioned_floor_area - foundation_floor_area * (total_conditioned_floors - floors_above_grade)`
where `foundation_floor_area` is the foundation zone's `floor_area_m2`, and mutate the conditioned
zone's `floor_area_m2` to this value. Both locals (`total_conditioned_floors`, `floors_above_grade`)
are already in scope at that point. Skip the subtraction when either count is `None` (slab/no basement).

---

### Finding 2 — MEDIUM: Conditioned zone volume uses inflated area (consequence of Finding 1)

**Location:** `building.rs:675`

```rust
ZoneType::Conditioned => zone.floor_area_m2.map(|a| a * default_height_m),
```

Until Finding 1 is fixed this multiplies the too-large `conditioned_floor_area` by ceiling height,
giving an over-estimated air volume for the conditioned zone. OCHRE uses `indoor_floor_area * ceiling_height`
(line 548). This is a direct downstream consequence of Finding 1 and is resolved by the same fix.

---

### Finding 3 — LOW: `total_conditioned_floors` not stored on `Building` struct

**Location:** `building.rs:423-431`

`total_conditioned_floors` is a local variable used only for the basement conditioned/unconditioned
heuristic (line 475) and then dropped. It is not stored on `Building` (only `floors_above_grade`
is stored, line 755). Once Finding 1 is fixed this local must also participate in the
`indoor_floor_area` computation. Because both locals are in scope at the same call site, no struct
change is strictly required, but the derivation should be encapsulated clearly in the code.

---

### Finding 4 — LOW: No cross-check between derived and boundary-summed floor area

OCHRE emits a warning when `indoor_floor_check` (sum of per-storey boundary areas) disagrees
with `indoor_floor_area` by more than 10 m² (`hpxml.py:484`). HARES has no equivalent guard.
After fixing Finding 1, add a `tracing::warn!` if the conditioned zone area from the formula
disagrees substantially from the sum of conditioned-zone floor/frame-floor boundary areas.

---

### Floor area derivation comparison table

| Aspect | OCHRE | HARES |
|---|---|---|
| Source field | `ConditionedFloorArea` (always ft²) | `ConditionedFloorArea` (unit attr handled) |
| Underground subtraction | `conditioned_floor_area - first_floor_area * (total - above_grade)` | **Missing** — raw value used |
| `first_floor_area` source | Area of lowest floor boundary (Floor/Foundation Floor/Raised Floor) | Not derived |
| Foundation zone area | `first_floor_area` from floor boundary | `<Foundation>/<FloorArea>` element |
| Zone area overlap | Non-overlapping | Conditioned and foundation overlap for basement buildings |
| Conditioned zone volume | `indoor_floor_area * ceiling_height` | `conditioned_floor_area * ceiling_height` (inflated) |
| Consistency warning | Yes (`abs(check - area) > 10 m²`) | None |
