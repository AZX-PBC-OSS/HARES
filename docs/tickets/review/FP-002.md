## Background/Context

In ideal mode, OCHRE scales fan power proportionally to the delivered capacity fraction.
If HARES doesn't do this, fan power may be over/underestimated.

## Investigation Instructions

1. Read OCHRE ideal mode fan power.
2. Read HARES `ideal_hvac.rs` — is fan power proportional to delivered capacity?
3. If fan power is missing from ideal mode: what's the impact on total kWh?

## Findings

### OCHRE behavior (vendors/OCHRE/ochre/Equipment/HVAC.py)

In ideal-capacity mode OCHRE scales fan power **proportionally to delivered electrical power**:

```python
# HVAC.py:517
def update_fan_power(self, capacity):
    if self.use_ideal_capacity:
        return capacity * self.eir * self.fan_power_ratio
```

where `fan_power_ratio` is established at init as:

```python
# HVAC.py:153
self.fan_power_ratio = self.fan_power_max / (self.capacity_max * self.eir_max)
```

Fan power is then added to zone heat delivery and to electrical consumption:

```python
# HVAC.py:543-554
self.delivered_heat = heat_gain * self.shr + self.fan_power   # sensible to zone
self.electric_kw    = power_kw + self.fan_power / 1000        # electrical draw
```

The formula is therefore **proportional to the load fraction**: if ideal capacity is 50 % of rated, fan power is also 50 % of rated fan power.

### HARES behavior (crates/hares-equipment/src/hvac/ideal_hvac.rs)

`IdealHvac` has **no fan power concept at all**:

- No field for rated fan power, `fan_power_ratio`, or per-flow-rate fan power.
- `step()` (lines 397-448) pushes only `capacity_w` as a `PortContribution::Thermal` and sets `tk::THERMAL_OUTPUT_W`. No electrical port contribution is emitted.
- No fan heat added to zone sensible gains.
- Telemetry fields: `THERMAL_OUTPUT_W`, `OPERATING_MODE`, `IDEAL_CAPACITY_W`, `CURRENT_TARGET_C` — no fan power key.

### Gap summary

| Dimension | OCHRE | HARES |
|---|---|---|
| Fan power formula (ideal mode) | `capacity * eir * fan_power_ratio` (proportional) | Not implemented |
| Fan heat to zone | Added to `delivered_heat` | Not added |
| Fan electrical consumption | Added to `electric_kw` | Not emitted |
| Telemetry | `Fan Power (kW)` at verbosity >= 7 | Absent |

### Impact

1. **Electrical consumption undercount**: Fan power is typically 5-15 % of total HVAC electric draw (rule-of-thumb: ~0.3 W per m³/h across typical residential duct systems). At ideal timesteps this entire component is zero in HARES.
2. **Zone thermal error**: OCHRE adds fan motor heat to sensible zone gains. HARES omits this, causing a small systematic cooling bias in heating-mode results and a small under-estimate of zone heat gain in all active modes.
3. **Coil inlet temperature error**: OCHRE also bumps `coil_input_db` by `fan_power_per_flow_rate / (rho * cp)` before computing SHR and biquadratic corrections. HARES has no equivalent, so SHR and EIR biquadratic corrections will differ slightly when a full HVAC model is wired to the ideal solver path.

### Recommended fix

Add to `IdealHvac`:

- `rated_fan_power_w: f64` config field (default 0.0; keep zero-fan path working for existing tests).
- `fan_power_ratio: f64 = rated_fan_power_w / (rated_capacity_w * rated_eir)` computed at `init()`.
- In `step()`: `fan_power_w = capacity_w.abs() * eir * fan_power_ratio` when on, else 0.
- Emit fan power as additional sensible zone gain (`PortContribution::Thermal` add-on) and as an electrical `PortContribution`.
- Add `tk::FAN_POWER_W` telemetry field.

The `eir` value must either be a stored field (rated EIR, defaulting to `1/cop` at rated conditions) or passed in as config. The proportional formula must respect the same `load_fraction` multiplier already applied to `capacity_w`.

## Review Notes

**Severity: HIGH** — `IdealHvac` emits zero fan power in all circumstances. OCHRE uses `capacity * eir * fan_power_ratio` (proportional to load fraction) for ideal mode. The gap covers both electrical consumption (missing from any port) and zone sensible heat addition (fan motor heat). This is a correctness defect with measurable energy-balance impact, not a style issue.
