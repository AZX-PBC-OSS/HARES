## Background/Context

OCHRE's `solve_ideal_capacity()` divides the solver-derived heat load by
`(shr + eir * fan_power_ratio)` for heating and `(shr - eir * fan_power_ratio)` for
cooling. This accounts for: (1) SHR — only the sensible fraction of capacity does
useful thermal work, and (2) fan power — fan motor heat adds to heating or subtracts
from cooling.

## Investigation Instructions

1. **Read OCHRE `solve_ideal_capacity()`:**
   - What is the exact formula? Document for both heating and cooling.
   - What are `shr`, `eir`, `fan_power_ratio`?

2. **Read HARES `ideal_hvac.rs` step():**
   - How is ideal capacity applied?
   - Is the solver-provided capacity adjusted for fan power and SHR?
   - Or is it used directly as sensible_gain_w?

3. **Known issue from exploration:**
   - HARES `ideal_hvac.rs` line ~425: `latent_gain_w = 0.0` — no latent cooling.
   - HARES uses `FuelType::Electric` always — no gas furnace ideal mode?
   - EndUse is always `HVAC_HEATING` even for cooling.

4. **Assess impact:**
   - How much error does omitting fan power adjustment introduce?
   - How much does omitting latent cooling matter in humid climates?

5. **Write findings** with formula comparison.


## Findings

### 1. OCHRE `solve_ideal_capacity` formula (HVAC.py lines 411-429)

OCHRE back-solves the envelope RC model for the heat load `h_desired` (W) required to
reach the setpoint, then adjusts for the fact that compressor capacity is not identical
to net zone heat delivered:

```
# heating
capacity = h_desired / (shr + eir * fan_power_ratio)

# cooling (h_desired is positive; returned value is negative = cooling)
capacity = -h_desired / (shr - eir * fan_power_ratio)
```

Definitions:
- `shr` — sensible heat ratio from the previous timestep (cooling only; 1.0 for heating).
- `eir` — energy input ratio (1/COP) from the previous timestep.
- `fan_power_ratio = fan_power_max / (capacity_max * eir_max)` — dimensionless fan-to-
  compressor-input-power ratio, computed once at init (HVAC.py line 153).

The fan power and SHR adjustments cancel the extra/missing zone heat:
- **Fan motor heat (heating):** fan shaft heat adds to the zone, so less compressor
  capacity is needed. The denominator is `shr + eir * fan_power_ratio` because fan heat
  is `capacity * eir * fan_power_ratio` and it is all sensible (SHR=1 for fan).
- **Fan motor heat (cooling):** fan shaft heat _opposes_ cooling, so the denominator
  shrinks to `shr - eir * fan_power_ratio`.
- **SHR (cooling):** only the sensible fraction of capacity does net zone-temperature
  work; latent capacity cools moisture, not air. Dividing by SHR inflates the required
  compressor capacity to compensate.

On the _delivery_ side (calculate_power_and_heat, line 543):
```
delivered_heat = heat_gain * shr + fan_power  # sensible to zone
latent_gain    = heat_gain * (1 - shr)        # latent to zone
```
So the zone receives `capacity * shr + fan_power` sensible watts, and `capacity * (1-shr)` latent watts.

### 2. HARES `IdealHvac` — no fan power or SHR adjustment

`ideal_hvac.rs` step() (lines 403-431):

```rust
let capacity_w = (self.ideal_capacity_w * self.load_fraction).max(0.0); // heating
// ...
ports.accumulate(&PortContribution::Thermal {
    sensible_gain_w: capacity_w,
    latent_gain_w: 0.0,          // always zero
    ...
})?;
```

The solver-provided `ideal_capacity_w` is passed through as `sensible_gain_w` with
`latent_gain_w = 0.0`. There is no fan power term added and no SHR divisor applied.

This means:
- **Fan power omitted (both heating and cooling).** For heating, the zone receives
  fewer watts than intended — the fan contributes heat that HARES does not model.
  For a typical 10 kW system (EIR≈0.33, fan_power_ratio≈0.05), the missing fan term is
  ~165 W sensible, or ~1.6% of nominal capacity.
- **SHR omitted (cooling).** HARES delivers `ideal_capacity_w` entirely as sensible
  heat, not `ideal_capacity_w * shr` sensible + `ideal_capacity_w * (1-shr)` latent.
  For SHR=0.75 in a humid climate, latent cooling is 25% of total capacity. The zone
  temperature and humidity both diverge.
- **Back-solve is also uncorrected.** `solve_ideal_capacity_for_target` computes raw
  sensible load from the RC model — equivalent to OCHRE's `h_desired`. OCHRE then
  divides by `(shr ± eir * fan_power_ratio)` to obtain the correct compressor capacity.
  HARES does not apply that correction, so the ideal capacity signal is already `h_desired`
  rather than the equipment capacity needed to produce `h_desired`.

  In aggregate, the error chain is: solver returns `h_desired` → passed directly as
  `ideal_capacity_w` → emitted as `sensible_gain_w = h_desired`. OCHRE would have
  computed `capacity = h_desired / (shr ± eir*fan_power_ratio)` and then delivered
  `capacity * shr + fan_power = h_desired` sensible. So for heating with `shr=1` and
  ignoring fan, the round-trip produces the correct sensible output for that case, but
  the _compressor capacity_ (and hence electrical draw) reported would be wrong. For
  cooling the SHR distortion is live.

### 3. `AirConditioner` ideal-capacity path (air_conditioner.rs lines 823-837)

In the AC path under `use_ideal`, the solver-provided `ideal_capacity_w` is used to
derive PLR from the biquadratic-corrected capacity. Fan shaft heat raises the coil
entering wet-bulb (lines 786-806), and `calculate_shr` returns the actual SHR. The
final zone contribution is `-sensible_cooling_w` and `-latent_cooling_w` (lines 639-640).
This path correctly handles fan heat and SHR — the finding in §2 is specific to
`IdealHvac`, not to the physics equipment.

### 4. Additional `IdealHvac` defects confirmed

- `EndUse::HVAC_HEATING` is hardcoded regardless of operating mode
  (`ideal_hvac.rs:75`). When cooling, telemetry reports heating end-use.
- `FuelType::Electric` is hardcoded (`ideal_hvac.rs:77`). Gas furnace or gas HP in
  ideal mode cannot report correct fuel.
- `latent_gain_w: 0.0` is always emitted — no latent cooling is delivered to the
  humidity solver. This causes humidity to be under-dehumidified in cooling mode.

### 5. Magnitude assessment

| Condition | Missing term | Error magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| Heating, typical (EIR=0.33, fan_ratio=0.05) | fan heat ≈ 1.6% of capacity | Small — well under 1°C MAE |
| Cooling, humid climate (SHR=0.75) | latent ~25% of total capacity mismatch | Large — incorrect humidity, room DB temp off by >1°C over hours |
| Cooling, dry climate (SHR=0.95) | latent ~5% | Moderate — small temperature error |

The SHR omission for cooling in humid climates is a **medium-severity correctness
defect** for `IdealHvac`. The fan power omission is low-severity for temperature
accuracy but affects electric consumption telemetry.

## Trace Summary (added after full code trace)

### What the solver returns

`ThermalSolver::solve_ideal_capacity_for_target` (`hares-envelope/src/thermal_solver/stepping.rs:24`)
back-solves the state-space RC model for the sensible input watt value that will move the zone
temperature from its current state to `target_c` in one timestep. This value is semantically
identical to OCHRE's `h_desired` — it is the net sensible watts the zone air node must receive.

This is NOT compressor capacity; it is the heat the zone air must see. The value is written
directly into `IdealHvac.ideal_capacity_w` (via `ControlSignal::IdealCapacity`) and then
emitted as `sensible_gain_w` into the thermal port, which feeds back into the solver's input
vector via `apply_port_sensible_inputs` (`hares-envelope/src/thermal_solver/ports.rs:19`).

**The round-trip is self-consistent for the thermal solver**, but the value is NOT decomposed
into sensible vs latent, and no electrical port contribution is emitted. Key gaps confirmed:

### Confirmed defects

**D1 (Medium — cooling humidity error):** `latent_gain_w` is always `0.0` in `IdealHvac::step()`
(`ideal_hvac.rs:428`). The humidity solver never receives latent cooling. This is a correctness
defect for humid-climate cooling simulations.

**D2 (Low — electric power not reported):** `IdealHvac` emits no `PortContribution::Electrical`.
The fan power and compressor power are structurally absent. The only electrical entry in
`PortSlots` is `Default::default()` (`ideal_hvac.rs:657`). This means electrical consumption
is silently zero in ideal HVAC mode.

**D3 (Low — fan heat omitted from zone):** For heating, fan shaft heat should add sensible
watts to the zone. For cooling, it opposes net cooling. Since `IdealHvac` has no
`fan_power_w_per_m3_s` field and emits no fan-derived thermal contribution, this term is absent.
Magnitude: ~1-2% of capacity for typical systems (see §5 of Findings above).

**D4 (Low — wrong EndUse in telemetry):** `EndUse::HVAC_HEATING` is hardcoded (`ideal_hvac.rs:75`).
Cooling cycles report a wrong end-use label in telemetry and aggregated metrics.

**D5 (Low — FuelType::Electric hardcoded):** `ideal_hvac.rs:77`. Gas furnace configurations in
ideal mode will report the wrong fuel. Affects `EnergyPlus`-compatible fuel-disaggregated output.

### Contrast with physics equipment

`AirConditioner::calculate_performance()` (`air_conditioner.rs:587-938`) correctly:
- Applies fan shaft heat to the entering coil wet-bulb temperature (`air_conditioner.rs:786-806`)
- Computes SHR via `calculate_shr` (the coil physics model)
- Splits capacity into `sensible_cooling_w` and `latent_cooling_w`
- Reports `fan_kw` and `compressor_kw` as separate electrical port contributions

`IdealHvac` is a simplified proxy; it is expected to trade physics fidelity for speed, but
the missing electrical and latent outputs are not documented as intentional omissions.

### Back-solve correction gap

OCHRE applies `capacity = h_desired / (shr ± eir * fan_power_ratio)` to convert from zone
sensible need to compressor capacity, then reconstructs `delivered = capacity*shr + fan_power`.
The round-trip yields exactly `h_desired` sensible to the zone.

HARES skips both halves. The solver returns `h_desired`, which is pushed as `sensible_gain_w`
directly. This is numerically equivalent to the correct sensible delivery for heating with
SHR=1 and zero fan power. For cooling with SHR < 1 (typical: 0.7–0.85) the zone sees the
full `h_desired` as sensible, but no latent — the humidity solver is starved of latent cooling,
and the zone temperature settles correctly at setpoint while the humidity drifts upward.

## Review Notes

`IdealHvac` hardcodes `EndUse::HVAC_HEATING` even when cooling — telemetry BUG. Hardcodes
`FuelType::Electric` — prevents gas furnace ideal mode from correct fuel reporting. The
`AirConditioner` ideal-capacity path is correctly implemented and is not affected by these
findings. `IdealHvac` is a solver-integrated lightweight proxy; the latent (D1) and electrical
(D2) omissions are the highest-priority gaps to address.
