## Background/Context

**NOT COVERED BY ANY EXISTING TICKET**: OCHRE reduces wall area by window and door area when processing attached openings (hpxml.py:109-133). When a window/door is attached to a wall, the wall's `Area` field is reduced by the opening's area. HARES does not do this, causing wall UA to be too high (wall area includes window/door area that should be excluded).

For example, if a wall has gross area 20 m² with a 2 m² window, the effective wall area should be 18 m². HARES currently uses 20 m², overstating heat loss through walls.

## Work to Do

- [ ] Track wall IDs during `parse_boundary` for `BoundaryType::Wall`:
  - Store wall areas in a `HashMap<String, f64>` mapping wall `SystemIdentifier` → `area_m2`
- [ ] In `parse_window` and `parse_door`, record `AttachedToWall` reference:
  - Each window/door has an `AttachedToWall/@idref` pointing to the wall it's attached to
  - Track these in a `Vec<(String, f64)>` mapping wall_id → opening_area
- [ ] After all boundaries are parsed, apply area reductions:
  - Iterate over window/door area records
  - Subtract opening area from the corresponding wall's area
  - Log a warning if wall area becomes negative (shouldn't happen with valid HPXML)
- [ ] Add unit tests:
  - Wall with one window: wall area = original - window area
  - Wall with multiple windows/doors: wall area = original - sum of opening areas
  - Wall with no openings: wall area unchanged
  - Window attached to non-existent wall: log warning, don't crash
