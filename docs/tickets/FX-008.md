## Background/Context

OCHRE processes `FrameFloors` as equivalent to `Floors`. HARES only processes `"Floors"`. Pier-and-beam or raised-floor homes silently drop their floor boundaries.

- [ ] Test both a raised-floor case (exterior = outdoor) and a crawlspace-ceiling case (exterior = foundation)
