## Background/Context

Water heaters have varying capabilities. All consume power (electric WH directly,
gas WH via fan/parasitics, HPWH via compressor). Gas WH and gas tankless also
consume fuel. All have operating mode. Tankless may be gas or electric depending
on configuration.

## Work to Do

For each water heater:
- [ ] Add `core_output: CoreOutput` field.
- [ ] Set `core_capabilities` in descriptor.
- [ ] Build local CoreOutput in step(), assign on success.
- [ ] Override `fn core_output(&self) -> &CoreOutput`.
- [ ] `core_output` is NOT persisted in checkpoints — recomputed on next step().

### Per-equipment:
| Equipment | ELECTRIC | FUEL | HAS_MODE |
|-----------|----------|------|----------|
| Resistance WH | Consumption | — | Yes |
| Gas WH | Consumption | Gas | Yes |
| HPWH | Consumption | — | Yes |
| Tankless (gas) | Consumption | Gas | Yes |
| Tankless (elec) | Consumption | — | Yes |

Note: Gas WH emits real electrical contribution (fan/parasitics) at
`gas.rs:479` via `PortContribution::Electrical`. This MUST be reported in
`core_output.flows.electric_kw` as `Consumption(fan_electric_w / 1000.0)`,
not `None`. Same for gas tankless (parasitic_electric_w). Omitting this
would undercount building electrical load.

## Measures of Success

- [ ] All water heaters override core_output()
- [ ] Gas WH reports fan electric draw as Consumption, NOT None
- [ ] Gas tankless reports parasitic electric draw as Consumption
- [ ] All existing WH tests pass
