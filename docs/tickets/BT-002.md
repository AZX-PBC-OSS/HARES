## Background/Context

In `distribute_transmitted_solar` (solar.rs:129-148), absorbed solar is split between the surface RC node and zone air using `radiation_frac`:
```rust
let to_node = absorbed * s.radiation_frac;
let to_air = absorbed * (1.0 - s.radiation_frac);
```

For high-R surfaces like BESTEST 900's concrete floor (R≈25 m²K/W), `radiation_frac ≈ 0.02`, routing 98% of floor-absorbed solar directly to zone air. This is physically wrong.

EnergyPlus deposits all solar radiation as a source term on the interior surface node. The heat then conducts to zone air through the normal convective heat transfer path (governed by the film coefficient). The existing `radiation_frac` is appropriate for **longwave radiation** (LWR) exchange between surfaces, where the voltage-divider model correctly partitions between the surface node and zone air. But solar radiation physically hits the surface — it doesn't bypass it.

After BT-001 corrects the rad_frac values themselves, this ticket fixes **how** solar uses them (i.e., it shouldn't).

## Work to Do

### solar.rs

- [ ] In `distribute_transmitted_solar` (lines 129-148), change the solar split to always deposit absorbed solar on the surface node:
  ```rust
  // Solar radiation is absorbed at the surface. The heat conducts to zone
  // air through the normal film resistance path. This matches EnergyPlus,
  // which treats solar as a source term on the interior surface node.
  for (s, &absorbed) in zone_cfg.surfaces.iter().zip(self.solar_absorbed_buf.iter()) {
      if s.input_index < u.len() && absorbed > 0.0 {
          u[s.input_index] += absorbed;
      }
  }
  ```

- [ ] Remove the `zone_air_gain` accumulation from the absorbed solar loop. The only contribution to zone air should be `reflected_w` (multi-bounce remainder that no surface absorbs).
