## Background/Context

Two physics-critical gaps:

1. **Infiltration (T3-5):** `apply_infiltration_and_ventilation` handles 5 `InfiltrationMethod` variants, duct leakage interaction, and natural ventilation. Only one smoke test exists (`density_is_dry_air_basis`). The main function has zero targeted tests.

2. **Humidity multi-zone (T3-6):** All 10 humidity solver tests are single-zone. The solver comment acknowledges "single-zone validated only." Multi-zone configurations (garage, attic, finished basement) may produce incorrect humidity transport.

- [x] Add humidity solver tests with 2+ zones verifying independent evolution and coupling

## Tests Added

**hares-envelope (infiltration.rs inline tests):**

Note: The `Aim2` variant does not exist in the codebase. The actual `InfiltrationMethod` enum variants are `AshraeWindStack`, `Ela`, and `Ach`. Tests were written against the actual API.

- `infiltration_ashrae_wind_stack_nominal` — `InfiltrationMethod::AshraeWindStack` with known NL, wind speed, stack coefficient. Assert flow rate within 5% of ASHRAE 136 hand calculation
- `infiltration_ashrae_wind_stack_zero_wind` — zero wind speed. Assert stack-only infiltration produces non-zero flow
- `infiltration_ela_nominal` — `InfiltrationMethod::Ela` at standard pressure differential. Assert flow rate matches ELA calculation
- `infiltration_constant_ach` — `InfiltrationMethod::Ach` with ACH=0.5. Assert flow rate = 0.5 × volume / 3600
- `infiltration_duct_leakage_interaction` — HVAC active with duct leakage. Assert total infiltration is reduced by the duct-leakage-to-outside fraction
- `infiltration_duct_leakage_hvac_off` — HVAC off with duct leakage config. Assert duct leakage does not affect infiltration
- `infiltration_natural_ventilation_open` — natural ventilation enabled with favorable conditions (indoor > outdoor temp). Assert ventilation flow is added to infiltration
- `infiltration_natural_ventilation_closed` — conditions unfavorable. Assert zero natural ventilation contribution
- `infiltration_latent_output` — non-zero humidity difference. Assert `latent_out` buffer receives correct moisture transport values
- `infiltration_energy_decomposition_sums` — assert `q_infiltration_w + q_forced_vent_w + q_natural_vent_w == q_sensible_diagnostic_w` within f64 epsilon

**hares-envelope (humidity_solver.rs inline tests):**

- `humidity_two_zone_independent_evolution` — two zones with different latent gains. Assert each zone's humidity ratio evolves independently per its own gain
- `humidity_two_zone_different_volumes` — two zones with different volumes. Assert larger zone has slower humidity response for same latent gain
- `humidity_zone_volume_override` — configure `zone_volumes_m3` override in `HumiditySolverConfig`. Assert overridden volume is used instead of `zone.volume_m3`
- `humidity_new_zone_fallback` — zone not present in initial `humidity_ratios`. Assert fallback to `zone.humidity_ratio` default
- `humidity_garage_boundary` — garage zone at outdoor conditions. Assert humidity ratio tracks closer to outdoor than indoor zone
