You were here to triage findings and decide what action to take — not to implement, fix, or write tests. Before you signal done, re-read your triage. Every answer wants a specific finding, a specific gap, or a specific reason no action is needed.

**Overreach — did you do a job that was not yours?** Name any fix you applied, any test you wrote, any implementation you touched. Your job is assessment and routing, not action.

**Shortfall — did you do the job below the bar?**

- If you declared action needed: name the specific findings that warrant follow-up and what should be done about each. "More work needed" is not a finding — name what is missing.
- If you declared no action needed: name every finding you assessed and why it doesn't warrant action. A finding dismissed without a reason will resurface.

**Abdication — did you really play the role?**

- State the strongest case for why action is needed even if you declared no action needed. If you cannot, you did not look hard enough.
- Name the riskiest unaddressed finding and what could go wrong if it's ignored. If you cannot name it, your triage was superficial.
