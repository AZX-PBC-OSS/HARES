## MAJOR-1 - Precomputed same-zone (party wall / furniture) path wires every resistor one index off; the equivalence test cannot discriminate
- `boundary_rc.rs:1797-1799` (`res_list.pop()`), `:1847` (`res_offset = 0` for same-zone), `:1851-1876` (wiring).
- After the same-zone split, the padded-average resistors for the kept interior half are `[r_start/2 (cut-surface stub), (r_start+r_start+1)/2, ..., r_{n-1}/2 (zone-facing half-layer)]`. The code pops the **last** resistor (the zone-facing half-layer) then wires with `res_offset = 0`, so the cut-surface stub becomes the node0-node1 resistor and every inter-layer resistor shifts one index toward the zone; the innermost node connects to the zone via `(r_{n-2}+r_{n-1})/2 + film` instead of `r_{n-1}/2 + film`. Total series R unchanged; capacitors sit one half-layer off, changing fin dynamics. The material-layer path (`:1599-1684`) is node-centered and exact - the two advertised-equivalent paths disagree.
- Measured with a probe on 4 identical layers (ScriptF, same-zone, 20 m2): material path inner-node-to-zone conductance 1.307e-3 vs precomputed 1.010e-3 (-23%); inner mass 10-minute response to a 1 K zone step 0.394 vs 0.293 (-25%).
- Why the test misses it: `material_and_precomputed_same_zone_step_response_match` (`:2919-3053`) steps 600 x 60 s (~40-70 time constants) to steady state, where both topologies converge to zone temperature regardless of resistor distribution; asserts 1e-6 agreement. Validates convergence, not dynamics. The comment at `:1727-1733` notes HARES reverses OCHRE's orientation but copies OCHRE's `res_list[:-1]` pop, which is correct only in OCHRE's orientation.
- Production impact masked today: same-zone LUT boundaries resolve to "Indoor/Foundation/Garage Furniture" rows, all single-layer in the shipped CSV (`vendors/OCHRE/ochre/defaults/Envelope/Envelope Materials.csv`); for n=1 both resistors are r/2. Live for any multi-layer furniture-like LUT row or TOML override.
- Fix: in the same-zone branch `res_list.remove(0)` (drop the cut stub) and wire with `res_offset = 0`. Add a discriminating test: 2-minute step response of both paths agrees to 1e-9 on a 4-layer two-material stack.

## MAJOR-2 - Zone-node IDs collide with layer-node IDs at n_zones >= 1000, silently overwriting zone capacitance
- `boundary_rc.rs:50` (`LAYER_NODE_BASE = 1000`), `:665-669` (zone nodes `1..=n_zones`), `:1526-1531` (`alloc_node` overwrites the `capacitances` entry for an existing ID). Nothing guards `n_zones < 1000`. Some wirings trip the loud `SelfLoopEdge` guard (`rc_network.rs:95-97`) but exterior-edge wirings corrupt silently.
- Fix: `assert!(n_zones < LAYER_NODE_BASE)` (or typed error) at the top of `assemble_building_rc`; make `ZoneId`'s `u16::try_from(idx+1).unwrap_or(u16::MAX)` a typed error (`environment.rs:1989`).

## MAJOR-3 - Exterior skin iteration converges by warm-start accretion at 1-minute timesteps; non-converged skins silently accepted
- `solver_builder.rs:1037` (`n_iter = floor(dt/300)+1` -> 1 at dt=60 s), `thermal_solver/longwave.rs:317-374`, +-2 K step clamp at `:324`, fixed-point invariant enforced only `if converged` (`:352-374`). At 60 s the skin gets one damped iteration from the warm start; mid-transient (sunrise, cloud passage) the skin lags its fixed point and the non-converged exit is accepted with no counter or diagnostic.
- Fix: keep n_iter but count iteration-cap exits and report max skin residual in `EnvelopeComponentGains` (or warn on first lag > 1 K). A 2-iteration Newton solve (linearize T^4 at the warm start) converges exactly in 1-2 iterations at equal cost.

## MINOR
- **m4.** Doc/code divergence: `thermal_solver/solar.rs:85` includes ground-reflected irradiance in window diffuse POA; `docs/thermal-envelope-solver.md:275` says "excluded for windows by design". Fix the doc (or record in DIVERGENCES).
- **m5.** `MIN_CAPACITANCE_J_K` floor (`boundary_rc.rs:31`, applied `:623`, `:1623`, `:1811`) silently inflates thin/small-area layers' mass by orders of magnitude. Merge sub-floor nodes into their neighbor (preserving the R-chain) or count in `EnvelopeDiagnostics`.
- **m6.** Per-step TARP convection correction is a one-sided ratchet (`stepping.rs:688-720`): only dh > 0 applied. `PerStepTarp` mode is "max(TARP, static)", not TARP. Production pins `AshraeSimple` so dormant; document or gate.
- **m7.** Reported interior convective loads clamp dT to >= 0.1 K (`stepping.rs:851-871`): small nonzero flux at near-equal temperatures and the upward-flow enhanced branch at the floor. Affects reported `wall/floor/roof_heat_gain_w` columns, not state. Handle dT -> 0 analytically.
- **m8.** `assert!` panics on physics paths: `solar.rs:100-105` (`shgc >= transmittance - 1e-6`). Construction-time typed check in `WindowSolarProperties` with the surface id.
- **m9.** Interior LWR silently skipped for single-surface zones (`longwave.rs:412-414`). One-time warn per zone (pattern exists at `:511-519`).
- **m10.** Silent uniform-temperature fallback when the no-pin steady-state solve fails (`initialization.rs:194-198`). Add `tracing::warn!`.
- **m11.** Same-zone material fin: doc text ("the middle layer's capacitance is halved", `thermal-envelope-solver.md:70`) describes only the odd case; even counts keep two full-capacitance half-layers whose cut-side half-resistances are dropped (physically exact). Doc nit.
- **m12.** Multi-depth ground support has an unwritten 99-depth ceiling: `boundary_rc.rs:42-48` (`GROUND_NODE_BASE = u32::MAX - 100`), `depth_to_node` indexes without bound check; >= 100 depths silently wrap. Guard in `unique_depths` construction (`:638-653`).
- **m13.** Diurnal layer split inflates state count for massive below-grade walls (`boundary_rc.rs:111-126`, `:1569-1593`): a 2.4 m concrete foundation wall -> ~31 sub-layers; four basement walls add ~120 states; dense DMatrix LU/Gershgorin/Pade scale with it. Cap the split (8-10) or share states across same-construction boundaries.
- **m14.** Window's outward-absorbed solar share vanishes from the budget (`solar.rs:106-117`): the `(1 - N_i)` share of `(SHGC - tau)` is deposited nowhere (should warm the glass and leave via exterior convection/LWR; the window has no exterior node). ~1-3% of incident. Document next to the SHGC decomposition or fold into the existing sky-deviation correction branch (`longwave.rs:74-149`).
- **m15.** Degraded ideal-capacity fallback is tracing-only (`stepping.rs:532-591`): after N consecutive solve failures the zone silently rides the last-good capacity. Expose `ideal_capacity_degraded_zones` in component_gains/run diagnostics so batch runs can fail a quality gate.

## Top 5 improvement opportunities
1. **Newton (semi-analytic) exterior skin solve** replacing the heavy-ball fixed point (`longwave.rs:321-335`) - one scalar equation in T_s^4; ~2 Newton steps to machine precision; removes the clamp, damping constants, and the lag. Effort M. Pinning tests (`exterior_skin_balance.rs`, `iterative_skin_temperature_satisfies_exact_skin_balance`) already encode the target.
2. **Unify the material and precomputed boundary builders into one node-centered path** (`boundary_rc.rs:1564-1879`) - both emit `(caps, node-centered half-resistances, optional floating surface node)`, one wiring function. ISO 13786 then validates one path. Effort M.
3. **Give windows a single glass temperature node** (E+ "Option 2" style) collapsing the outward-absorbed share (m14), the sky-deviation correction (`longwave.rs:74-149`), and the interior LWR special case (`longwave.rs:460-475`) into one skin elimination via the existing `skin_rad_coupling` machinery (`boundary_rc.rs:215-230`). Effort M-L.
4. **Cache the environment-derived half of `build_input_vector`** - it runs twice per step (prepare + integrate, `stepping.rs:734-762`, `:765-783`), paying the skin iteration, Kusuda, solar distribution, infiltration twice. Split `u` into env-dependent (computed once, invalidated by env change) and port-dependent (rebuilt in integrate) segments. Bitwise-safe: the integrate pass overwrites everything it reads. Largest remaining per-step win I-08 left on the table. Effort M.
5. **Star-mesh interior LWR: per-construction zone operating point** instead of the 20 C fixed linearization (`rc_network.rs:902-945` documents -4% to +12% h_rad error, worst for sunlit roofs and cold windows - free-float BESTEST regimes). Bake at the zone's expected MRT from `initialization.rs`. Effort S.

## Validation gaps
- A same-zone transient test that can discriminate resistor distribution (short-window <= 5 min response at 1e-9 on a two-material stack, or hand-computed A-matrix entries).
- ISO 13786 dynamic thermal characteristics (areal heat capacity, decrement factor, phase lag) per construction - validates `split_layer_count` and the node-centered discretization dynamically.
- BESTEST coverage: only 600, 900, 600FF, 900FF, 640 run (`tests/bestest/mod.rs:194-300`). Missing 620 (window/shading - IAM + SHGC decomposition), 650 (airflow x envelope), 920/950 (high-mass with/without night ventilation - layer-split mass), 195/395 (ground coupling). ASHRAE 140 peak-load bands pinned in-code.
- Timestep-sweep invariance: free-float zone temps at dt = 60/300/900/3600 s, monotone convergence (would have quantified MAJOR-3's lag).
- Ground-coupling analytical check: Kusuda-Achenbach at 0.5 m vs the analytic series; multi-depth ground columns depth-matched through `resolve`.
- Window angular transmittance pinned at 40/60/80 deg vs the E+ reference table (`hares-physics/src/solar.rs:2065+` pins only normal-incidence unity and diffuse integration).
- Season-long whole-run invariant: `through_glass - window_out == sum(injections)` (the `solver_energy_conservation.rs` harness could host it).

**Overall:** the solver core is genuinely strong - deterministic Kirchhoff assembly, textbook ZOH, exact eliminated-skin algebra, silent-fallback class nearly extinct. The two real defects are both in the same-zone precomputed corner and its validation blind spot.
